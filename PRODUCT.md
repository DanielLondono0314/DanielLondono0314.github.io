# PRODUCT.md — Portfolio Daniel Londoño

**register:** brand

## Product Purpose
Personal portfolio for Daniel Londoño, vibecoder / AI App Builder / software engineering student from Medellín, Colombia. The portfolio IS the product: its job is to convert visitors (recruiters, hiring managers, potential clients) into interviews and contracts. Primary goal: land work with US companies (English-speaking market).

## Users
- US tech recruiters and hiring managers scanning dozens of portfolios; give them signal in under 30 seconds.
- Potential freelance clients (small business owners like Michael Cornell) who judge by visual taste and shipped results.
- Spanish-speaking local market (secondary).

## Brand & Tone
- "Vibe Logic Light" design system: off-white (#faf8ff), navy ink (#131b2e), acid green (#d4ff00) accent, hard shadows, Bricolage Grotesque + Inter + JetBrains Mono.
- High-energy studio feel: technical precision + creative flair. Confident, direct, no filler.
- Differentiator: builds REAL shipped products with AI tooling (Lovable, Claude, React), not tutorial clones. Every project solves a concrete problem, several have real users/payments (Wompi integration, live ecommerce).

## Anti-references
- Generic dark "hacker" portfolios with purple gradients.
- Template-feeling portfolios (cookie-cutter cards, stock icons).
- Buzzword-stuffed resumes with no shipped proof.

## Strategic principles
- Proof over claims: live previews, real URLs, real payment integrations beat skill bars.
- Bilingual EN/ES with EN as the conversion language for US market.
- Zero build step (GitHub Pages), performance and accessibility are part of the pitch.
